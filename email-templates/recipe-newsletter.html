Some mornings ask for something that feels like a treat and still looks after you. This is that breakfast: crisp sweet-potato and carrot patties, crowned with a rose of smashed avocado.

The Rebel Avocado Toast

The patties are steamed, shaped and chilled in the freezer for 20 minutes. That little pause firms them up, so they turn golden and crisp with just a spoonful of ghee or olive oil. Mint-coriander chutney and a squeeze of lemon bring the freshness.

In Ayurveda, this plate is gentle on Agni, balancing for Vata and Pitta, and kind to Kapha at breakfast or lunch. Modern nutrition agrees: fibre plus healthy fats means steadier blood sugar and a calmer afternoon. And yes, the full recipe is free.
