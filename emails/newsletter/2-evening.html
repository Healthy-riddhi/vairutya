A few days ago, we looked at how changing the sequence of your morning coffee can protect your energy. Today, let's talk about the other half of the equation: how you end your day.

Ever go to bed early, sleep 8 full hours, and still wake up feeling like you barely rested?

Usually, the culprit isn't how long you sleep. It's what your body was busy doing while you were unconscious. When you eat heavy meals or sugary treats right before bed, your system spends the first half of the night digesting instead of repairing.

Here is your Evening Micro-Hack:

The Micro-Hack: The 10-Minute Post-Dinner Stroll

Standard eveningThe Vairutya Evening Shift
Couch right after dinner: Glucose settles in your bloodstream, leading to night sweats and restless sleep.10-minute light movement: Take a brief walk, do gentle stretches, or do light tidying right after eating.
Result: Elevated heart rate during sleep, waking up groggy.Result: Muscles soak up excess glucose, leading to deeper REM sleep.

Why This Works

Your muscles are your body's biggest glucose sponges. When you contract them lightly after a meal, even just by walking around the living room or clearing the kitchen, they absorb energy directly from your blood without needing extra insulin.

This lowers your body temperature faster, allowing you to slip into deep, restorative sleep much earlier in the night. No intense workouts required.

Try This Tonight

After your final meal today, set a timer for 10 minutes and just move lightly. You don't need gym clothes or a workout plan. Just keep moving before you settle into the couch or bed.

Notice how you feel when your alarm goes off tomorrow morning!
