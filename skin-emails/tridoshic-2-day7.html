Eat for the season: Maintaining your Tridoshik glow from within

Dear {{ contact.FIRSTNAME }},

For Tridoshik skin, your greatest superpower is adaptability. Because your doshas are naturally balanced, your diet can be balanced too—rotating through all six Ayurvedic tastes (sweet, sour, salty, pungent, bitter, and astringent) depending on the season.

The Tridoshik Seasonal Rule of Thumb:

  • In Winter/Dry Weather: Favor warm, nourishing, slightly unctuous foods (soups, root vegetables, pure ghee) to keep Vata dryness at bay.
  • In Summer/Hot Weather: Favor cooling hydration (fresh fruits, cucumber, coconut water, mint) to keep Pitta heat pacified.
  • In Spring/Damp Weather: Favor lighter, spiced, and pungent meals to prevent sluggish Kapha accumulation.

By listening to the season and matching your plate to the weather outside, your skin stays effortlessly clear and glowing without needing aggressive corrections.
