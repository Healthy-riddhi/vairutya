Dear {{ contact.FIRSTNAME }},

{% if contact.SKIN_TYPE == "vata" %}

As noted in your Vata skin profile, your internal digestion and circulation play a massive role in how radiant your skin feels. When we eat raw, cold, or dry foods, it aggravates the Air element internally—manifesting as dehydration on the surface.

To restore a dewy, plump glow, your skin thrives on warm, moist, and grounding meals.

Try This Week: Golden Carrot & Ginger Coconut Stew

  • Why it works: Root vegetables grown underground (like carrots and sweet potatoes) carry earthy, grounding energy that pacifies Vata. Combined with healthy fats like pure cow’s ghee or coconut milk, it delivers deep internal lubrication to dry skin tissues.
  • The Ritual: Enjoy this bowl warm, seasoned with a pinch of black pepper and freshly grated ginger to ignite your digestive fire (agni) and boost micro-circulation to your face.

Skip the raw salads and iced drinks this week, and notice how much softer and more settled your skin feels from the inside out.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "pitta" %}

For Pitta-dominant skin, what happens in your stomach shows up on your face within hours. Excessively spicy, sour, fermented, or fried foods directly stoke your internal digestive fire (agni), leading to heat-induced flushing, irritation, and blemishes.

To keep your complexion clear and even-toned, your skin thrives on sweet, bitter, and astringent flavors paired with naturally cooling hydration.

Try This Week: Coconut, Cucumber & Mint Cooling Elixir Bowl

  • Why it works: Cucumber and fresh coconut are two of Ayurveda’s premier natural coolants, pulling excess heat out of the blood and liver (Rakta Dhatu, the tissue directly responsible for skin clarity). Fresh mint and coriander calm digestion without generating heat.
  • The Ritual: Blend peeled cucumber, tender coconut water, fresh mint leaves, a handful of soaked pumpkin seeds, and a pinch of roasted cumin powder into a chilled-at-room-temperature gazpacho. Drizzle with a teaspoon of pure cow’s ghee or cold-pressed coconut oil.

Skip raw onions, chilies, and extra coffee this week, and notice how much calmer and brighter your skin looks by Sunday.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "kapha" %}

Kapha skin is heavily influenced by heavy, cold, and sweet foods. Dairy, cold desserts, heavy oils, and iced drinks slow down your digestive fire (agni), producing excess mucus and sluggish metabolic waste (ama) that settles directly into skin tissues as congestion and dullness.

To keep your complexion light and clear, your skin thrives on pungent, bitter, and astringent flavors paired with warming spices.

Try This Week: Spiced Roasted Root Vegetables with Turmeric & Black Pepper

  • Why it works: Spices like ginger, black pepper, cinnamon, and turmeric act as natural internal decongestants. They boost micro-circulation, thin out stagnant lymphatic fluids, and fire up your metabolism to clear dull skin from the inside out.
  • The Ritual: Roast bitter greens (like methi or spinach) and root vegetables tossed in mustard oil or a tiny touch of ghee, heavily spiced with cumin, turmeric, and fresh ginger.

Skip dairy, heavy cheeses, and sugary snacks this week, and notice how much lighter and more clarified your skin feels.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "vata-pitta" %}

For Vata-Pitta skin, digestion is everything. Skip a meal and Vata creates internal dryness; eat something overly spicy, sour, or fried and Pitta sparks immediate facial redness.

Your complexion thrives on sweet, cooling, and unctuous (naturally moist) foods—flavors that ground scattered air while gently extinguishing excess heat.

Try This Week: Cardamom & Rose Stewed Pears with Ghee

  • Why it works: Cooked pears are deeply hydrating and easy on sensitive digestion, pacifying Vata dryness without feeling heavy. Pure cow’s ghee delivers cooling healthy fats directly to skin tissues, while crushed green cardamom and dried rose petals soothe Pitta heat.
  • The Ritual: Gently simmer sliced ripe pears in a splash of water with a teaspoon of pure ghee, crushed cardamom pods, and a pinch of edible rose petals until tender. Enjoy warm as a nourishing breakfast or afternoon reset.

Minimize raw salads, chilies, and skipped meals this week, and watch your skin barrier visibly soften and calm.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "vata-kapha" %}

Vata-Kapha skin is heavily impacted by cold and damp conditions. Heavy dairy, iced drinks, cold raw salads, and excessive sweets aggravate Kapha stagnation while simultaneously stirring up Vata dryness.

Your complexion thrives on light, warm, and pungent foods spiced generously to ignite your digestion and stimulate micro-circulation.

Try This Week: Spiced Red Lentil Dal with Ginger & Ghee

  • Why it works: Red lentils (masoor dal) are nourishing yet light, providing grounding plant protein without the heaviness of dairy or heavy grains. Tempered with warming ghee, fresh ginger, cumin, and black pepper, it stimulates lymphatic flow while deeply nourishing dry tissue layers.
  • The Ritual: Enjoy this warm and spiced soup for lunch or an early dinner, avoiding cold leftovers or chilled beverages.

Skip dairy, cold snacks, and iced drinks this week, and notice how much clearer and more energized your skin feels.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "pitta-kapha" %}

Pitta-Kapha skin is heavily aggravated by heavy dairy, fried foods, excessive oils, and sharp, fiery spices. These foods create sticky metabolic waste (ama) that mixes with excess heat, manifesting directly as skin congestion and breakouts.

Your complexion thrives on bitter, astringent, and naturally cooling foods that purify the blood and lighten sluggish tissue.

Try This Week: Bitter Melon & Moong Dal Detox Soup

  • Why it works: Bitter greens and bitter melon (karela) are premier blood purifiers in Ayurveda, pulling heat and toxins out of the liver while regulating sebum production. Split yellow mung dal provides light, oil-free protein that is easy on digestion.
  • The Ritual: Simmer yellow mung dal with fresh turmeric, coriander powder, and finely sliced bitter greens. Enjoy warm to cleanse the lymphatic channels and cool internal inflammation.

Skip heavy cheeses, oily snacks, and chilies this week, and watch your skin's clarity and texture visibly improve.

{% elif contact.SKIN_TYPE == "tridoshic" %}

For Tridoshik skin, your greatest superpower is adaptability. Because your doshas are naturally balanced, your diet can be balanced too—rotating through all six Ayurvedic tastes (sweet, sour, salty, pungent, bitter, and astringent) depending on the season.

The Tridoshik Seasonal Rule of Thumb:

  • In Winter/Dry Weather: Favor warm, nourishing, slightly unctuous foods (soups, root vegetables, pure ghee) to keep Vata dryness at bay.
  • In Summer/Hot Weather: Favor cooling hydration (fresh fruits, cucumber, coconut water, mint) to keep Pitta heat pacified.
  • In Spring/Damp Weather: Favor lighter, spiced, and pungent meals to prevent sluggish Kapha accumulation.

By listening to the season and matching your plate to the weather outside, your skin stays effortlessly clear and glowing without needing aggressive corrections.
